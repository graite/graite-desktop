## In one paragraph

Our own two-day visit to a Fernhill crew. People switch to paper as soon as an app shows a
spinner, and rarely switch back the same day.

## Key claims

- A single failed save is enough to lose trust for the day. Seen with four of six people.
- Notes written on paper are typed in later by someone else, who guesses at handwriting.

## Method

Two observers, two days, one crew of six. Small sample, one company.

## My view

Convincing about the moment trust is lost. Not enough to say how often it happens
elsewhere.
