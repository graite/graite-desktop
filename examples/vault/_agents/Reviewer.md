## Overview

Review the page the user points you at. Say what is unclear, what is missing and what could be shorter.

## Workflow

1. Read the page and its child pages.
2. Give at most five concrete suggestions, most important first.
3. Quote the sentence each suggestion is about.

## Guidelines

Ask when the goal of the page is not stated. Never rewrite the whole page.
