  This page is the product roadmap. Keep it short enough to read in two minutes.

  ## Structure
  - Three sections: **Now** (being built), **Next** (decided, not started) and **Later** (likely, not committed). No dates in Later.
  - Every item is one line: what the user gets, not what we build. It links to its spec subpage when there is one.
  - Each item has a priority score from RICE: reach, impact, confidence and effort. Show the four numbers, not only the result.

  ## How to help
  - When I add an idea, ask for the problem it solves and who asked for it, then propose a section and a score. Low confidence is a valid answer; say what would raise it.
  - When something moves between sections, note it under **Changes** with the date and the reason.
  - Before a planning session, list: items in Now for longer than six weeks, items without a spec, and items whose evidence is older than three months.
  - Challenge the list when everything is high priority. Ask what we would drop.

  ## Updates for others
  When I ask for a stakeholder update, write at most 150 words: what shipped, what is next, what changed and why, and what we need from the reader.
