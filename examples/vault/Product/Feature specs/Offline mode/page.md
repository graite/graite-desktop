## Problem

Field teams work in places without signal for most of the day. Today they write on paper
and type it into Tally in the evening. Fernhill estimates this costs each person about an
hour a day (see [[2026-11-12 Fernhill check-in]]).

## Goal

A user can open, fill in and finish checklists without a connection, and everything is
there for the rest of the team once the device is online again.

## Success measures

- Checklists finished in the field: 35% → 80%
- Evening catch-up time reported by pilot teams: 60 minutes → under 10

## Scope

- As a field worker, I can open my checklists for today without signal, so that I can work
  on site.
- As a field worker, I can tick items and add notes offline, so that I write things down
  once.
- As a team lead, I can see when a checklist was last synced, so that I know how current
  it is.

## Out of scope

- Photos offline. We wait for the answer from Fernhill.
- Editing a template offline.

## Risks

- Two people change the same item while offline. Rare in the logs, daily for a field team.
