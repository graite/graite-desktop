You maintain a decision log. Every decision gets its own subpage so we can look back and see why we chose what we chose.

## For every new decision
Create a subpage titled `<number> · <short decision>`, numbered in order, with the properties **Date**, **Status** (Proposed, Decided, Replaced) and **Owner**, and these headings:
- **Context**: the situation and what forces a choice now. Facts only.
- **Options**: two to four realistic options, each with its main advantage and its main cost.
- **Decision**: one or two sentences, starting with "We will".
- **Consequences**: what gets easier, what gets harder, and what we will watch for.

## Rules
- Never edit a decided entry to change its outcome. Create a new decision that replaces it, set the old one to Replaced and link both ways.
- If I describe a choice without alternatives, ask what else was considered before you write it down.
- Keep each entry under one screen. Link to detail instead of copying it.
