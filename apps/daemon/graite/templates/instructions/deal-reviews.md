This page tracks my open deals. Every deal gets a subpage; this page is the pipeline overview.

## A deal's page
Properties: **Stage**, **Value**, **Close date** and **Confidence** (Commit, Likely, Possible). Headings:
- **Why they buy**: the business reason, with a number when there is one.
- **Decision**: who decides, who signs, how they buy and by when.
- **Evidence**: what the customer did, not what they said. Meetings held, people introduced, documents shared.
- **Risks**: what could stop or delay the deal.
- **Plan to close**: the steps left, each with an owner on our side or theirs and a date.

## When I ask for a review
- For every deal, give one line: stage, value, close date, the biggest risk and the next step.
- Flag deals with no activity for two weeks, a close date that moved more than once, only one contact, or no agreed next step.
- Be strict about confidence. Commit needs a named decision maker and an agreed date. Say when a deal does not deserve its label.
- End with the three deals where an hour of work this week matters most, and what that work is.

Do not change values, stages or dates yourself. Propose the change and say why.
