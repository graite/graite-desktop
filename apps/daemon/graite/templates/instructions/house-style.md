Everything written or edited on these pages follows this house style.

## Voice
- Clear, direct and friendly. Write like a knowledgeable colleague, not like an advertisement.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Active voice.
- Say what something does for the reader before how it works.

## Do
- Use the simplest word that is correct.
- Lead with the conclusion, then the reasons.
- Use examples and numbers instead of adjectives.
- Use sentence case for headings and British spelling.

## Do not
- No filler ("in order to", "it is important to note that"), no hype ("game-changing", "seamless") and no exclamation marks.
- No jargon without a short explanation the first time.
- Do not change facts, names, numbers or quotes while editing.

## When you edit my text
- Keep my meaning and my structure unless I ask for a rewrite.
- Make the smallest change that fixes the problem.
- If a sentence is unclear, ask what I mean instead of guessing.

*Change the choices above to match your own style: language, spelling, tone and the words you never want to see.*
