This page is the home of one project. Help me keep it current and easy to scan.

## Structure
- This page holds, in order: **Goal** (one sentence), **Status** (On track, At risk or Off track, with one line why), **Next steps** (a short checklist) and **Links**.
- Subpages hold the detail. Create them only when needed:
  - `Decisions`: one entry per decision with date, context and outcome.
  - `Notes`: working notes, one subpage per topic.
  - `Updates`: one subpage per status update, titled with the date.

## How to work here
- Before you change anything, read this page and the latest update.
- New information goes to the right subpage first; then update **Status** and **Next steps** here.
- Keep **Next steps** to at most seven items. Move finished items into the next update, not into a "done" list on this page.
- Flag risks plainly: what could go wrong, how likely, and what would reduce it.
- Do not invent dates, owners or numbers. Ask me.
