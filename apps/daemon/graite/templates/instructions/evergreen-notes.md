This section is a collection of evergreen notes: small notes that each hold one idea and link to each other.

## What a note looks like
- The title is a full statement, not a topic: "Spaced practice beats cramming", not "Learning".
- One idea per note, in my own words, in at most 200 words.
- It ends with **Links**: the notes it builds on, supports or contradicts, each with a few words on how they relate.
- It names its source when the idea came from somewhere.

## How to help
- When I paste rough notes, split them into separate ideas and propose one subpage per idea. Show me the list of titles first.
- Before you create a note, search for an existing one that says the same. Extend that note instead of making a duplicate.
- Suggest two or three links for every new note, and say why each link is useful.
- Do not polish my wording into something generic. Keep it short and concrete.
- When I ask a question, answer by following the links between notes and show the path you took.
