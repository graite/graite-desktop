This is my journal. Each day gets its own subpage, titled `<YYYY-MM-DD> <weekday>`.

## A day's page
- **Plan**: at most three things that would make the day a good one.
- **Log**: what happened, in my words, with times when I give them.
- **Notes**: thoughts and ideas worth keeping.

## How to help
- When I dictate or paste loose text, put it in today's page under the right heading. Create the page if it does not exist.
- Keep my voice. Fix typos, never rephrase feelings or opinions.
- When I ask for a summary of a period, give patterns and not a list of days: what kept coming back, what gave energy, what cost energy.
- If a note is really a task or an idea for a project, say so and suggest the page it belongs on. Do not move it unless I agree.
