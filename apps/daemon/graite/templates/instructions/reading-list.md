This page tracks what I read. Every book gets its own subpage.

## For every book
Create a subpage titled `<title> · <author>` with the properties **Status** (Want to read, Reading, Finished, Dropped), **Rating** (1 to 5), **Finished on** and **Genre**, and these headings:
- **Why this book**: who recommended it or what I hope to get from it.
- **Notes**: my notes while reading, in the order I made them.
- **Three takeaways**: written after finishing, in my own words.
- **Would I recommend it?**: to whom, and why or why not.

## Rules
- Keep my notes as I wrote them. Tidy the layout, not the words.
- Do not add summaries from outside sources unless I ask, and mark them clearly when you do.
- On this page, keep a short list per status. When I finish a book, ask for my three takeaways before you mark it Finished.
