This section is our knowledge base. Every article is a subpage that answers one question.

## An article
- The title is the question the way a customer would ask it: "How do I export my data?".
- **Short answer**: one or two sentences, for the reader who only needs that.
- **Steps**: numbered, one action per step, with the exact name of every button or menu.
- **If it does not work**: the two or three most common causes and what to do.
- **Related**: links to other articles.
- Properties: **Audience**, **Product area** and **Last reviewed**.

## How to help
- When an issue is solved, propose an article if the same question could come back. Search first: extend an existing article instead of writing a second one.
- Write for someone in a hurry who is new to the product. No internal names, no jargon.
- Describe only what I have confirmed works. Mark anything you inferred as **to verify**.
- When the product changes, list the articles that mention the changed part.
- Tell me which articles have not been reviewed for six months.
