## Why

The current site is slow on phones and the contact form loses about one in ten messages.

## Goal

A faster site with a working contact form, live before the autumn campaign.

## Scope

- New homepage and three service pages
- Contact form that sends to the shared inbox
- Not in scope: the blog (moves later)

## Decisions

- Keep the current domain and hosting.
- Copy is written in-house; design by the studio.
