How we move a deal from first contact to a signed contract. Adjust the stages and probabilities to how your team sells; the deal table's **stage** options are the same list.

## Stages

| Stage       | Means                                        | Typical probability |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Lead        | We know about them; no conversation yet      | 10%                 |
| Qualified   | They have the problem, budget and a timeline | 25%                 |
| Proposal    | They have our proposal                       | 50%                 |
| Negotiation | Terms are being discussed                    | 75%                 |
| Won         | Signed                                       | 100%                |
| Lost        | Not going ahead; note why in _lost reason_   | 0%                  |

## Qualify a lead

- [ ] Is there a real problem we solve?
- [ ] Who decides, and who else is involved?
- [ ] Is there a budget, roughly?
- [ ] When do they need it?
- [ ] What happens if they do nothing?

## After every conversation

1. Open the deal and add an activity to its Activities list: Call, Email or Meeting, a short title and the details. Link it to the company and contact too.
2. Add the **next step** as a Task with a due date, so nothing goes stale.
3. Move the deal's stage and probability if it changed.

## Email templates

> [!toggle]- Follow-up after a first call
> Hi {name},
>
> Thanks for the time today. As promised, a short summary of what we discussed and the next step we agreed on: …
>
> Talk soon,

> [!toggle]- Sending a proposal
> Hi {name},
>
> Attached is our proposal for … It covers scope, timeline and price. I suggest we go through it together on …; does that time work for you?

> [!toggle]- Checking in on a quiet deal
> Hi {name},
>
> I wanted to check in on … Is this still a priority for you this quarter, or should we pick it up later?
