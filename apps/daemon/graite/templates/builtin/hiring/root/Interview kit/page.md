Use the same questions and the same scorecard for every candidate for a role, so scores can be compared.

## Before

- [ ] Read the CV and the notes from earlier rounds
- [ ] Agree who asks what

## Questions

1. Tell us about a piece of work you are proud of. What was your part?
2. Describe a time you disagreed with a colleague. What happened?
3. How would you approach the first month in this role?
4. What would you like to learn here?

## Scorecard

| Score | Means                               |
| ----- | ----------------------------------- |
| 5     | Clearly above what the role needs   |
| 4     | Meets everything, some things above |
| 3     | Meets what the role needs           |
| 2     | Gaps we would have to close         |
| 1     | Not a fit for this role             |

## After

- [ ] Log the interview with a score and a recommendation within a day
- [ ] Write notes on a page and link it from the interview
